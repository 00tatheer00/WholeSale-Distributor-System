1 Wholesale ERP vs. Retail Pharmacy POS

PharmaDist is an enterprise wholesale distribution ERP explicitly engineered for authorized pharmaceutical stockists, distributors, and bulk medicine suppliers. It enforces business mechanics distinct from retail pharmacy counters:

Operational Dimension Retail Point-of-Sale (POS) PharmaDist Wholesale ERP
Customer Profile Walk-in individual patients (loose strips, single bottles). Licensed Medical Stores, Pharmacies, Hospital Formularies.
Batch Tracking Optional or generic SKU-level tracking. Mandatory Batch Tracking tied to every carton.
Inventory Queue FIFO (First-In, First-Out) or unmanaged shelf picking. Strict FEFO (First-Expire, First-Out) auto-prioritization.
Credit Governance Immediate cash payment / instant card tap. Credit Limit Barrier Engine with approval workflows.
Documentation Thermal roll paper receipts (80mm / 58mm). DRAP Form 2-A Tax Invoices & Delivery Challans (A4).

3 Step-by-Step Business Setup & Daily Operations

1
Enterprise Profile & DRAP License Configuration Settings → General

Navigate to /settings. Enter your official Registered Wholesale Firm Name, DRAP Wholesale Drug License No. (e.g., DRAP/WDL/LHR-4921), NTN, STRN, primary warehouse address, and bank account credentials. These values automatically populate all printed Tax Invoices and Delivery Challans.

2
Warehouse Godown & Rack Architecture Inventory → Warehouses

Configure physical storage facilities. Establish your Main Central Godown and designate rack coordinates (e.g., Rack A-01 (Antibiotics), Cold Chain Unit #2 (Insulins)). Every subsequent purchase automatically pins batches to exact rack locations for rapid order picking.

3
Manufacturers & Supplier Principal Registry Purchases → Suppliers

Register licensed pharmaceutical manufacturing principals (e.g., GlaxoSmithKline, Abbott Laboratories, Getz Pharma, Searle, Highnoon). Record company sales contacts, supplier NTN, payment credit terms (e.g., 30 Days Net), and opening balances.

4
Medicine Master Formulary Registration Medicines → Catalog

Create master medicine profiles with Generic salt names, dosage forms (Tablet, Capsule, Syrup, Injectable), Trade Price (TP), Maximum Retail Price (MRP), wholesale discount percentages, and minimum reorder alert levels.

5
Factory Consignment Intake & Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Purchases → New Consignment

When receiving medicine shipments from pharma factories, select the Supplier, enter the Delivery Challan Number, and input individual batch numbers, manufacturing dates, expiry dates, purchase unit rates, and rack assignments. Upon submission, inventory quantities and Accounts Payable liabilities update immediately.

⚠️ FEFO Invariant Guardrail: Never enter ambiguous or dummy expiry dates during purchase intake. The ERP's automated batch picker relies exclusively on exact calendar dates to enforce DRAP First-Expire First-Out dispatch.
PharmaDist ERP — Sales Execution & Financial Recovery Page 4
6
Customer Pharmacies & Credit Limit Governance Customers → Directory

Register client pharmacies, clinics, and hospital procurement departments. Enter Pharmacy Drug License numbers, owner contacts, geographic sales routes, and strict Credit Limits (e.g., Rs. 300,000 with a 15-day maximum credit duration).

7
Sales Representatives & Field Order Bookers Sales Reps → Teams

Setup territory sales officers and order booking agents. Assign designated geographic routes and monthly sales recovery targets for commission and performance evaluations.

8
Wholesale Order Booking with Strict FEFO Dispatch Sales → New Wholesale Order

Select target Pharmacy. The real-time Credit Gauge displays existing outstanding balance against credit ceiling. When adding medicines, the system automatically pulls the earliest expiring valid batch. If customer's balance exceeds their credit limit, an approval barrier is enforced.

9
DRAP Form 2-A Tax Invoices & Delivery Challan Dispatch Invoices → Print Engine

Click Print Invoice to generate standard single-page A4 wholesale documents with complete batch tracking, Trade Price, MRP, warranty clauses, and authorized signatures. Click Print Challan to generate warehouse dispatch notes without commercial valuations for delivery drivers.

10
Market Recovery Collections & FIFO Settlement Payments → New Collection

When field agents return with market collections, record amounts against Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer, or Raast. The FIFO allocation engine automatically clears the oldest pending invoices first and generates an official printable Money Receipt (RCT).

✨ Historical Financial Integrity: Invoices permanently capture the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) at the exact moment of sale. Even if factory purchase prices fluctuate in future months, historical profit calculations remain 100% auditable.

5 Operational Troubleshooting FAQ

Q: Order displays "Stock Depleted"?

Remedy: The selected batch has reached zero quantity. Create a new purchase consignment via Purchases → New or verify stock transfers.

Q: "Credit Limit Exceeded" Warning?

Remedy: The customer has unpaid dues exceeding their allowed ceiling. Collect market payments or require Sales Manager credit override approval.

Q: Can expired batches be billed?

Remedy: Strictly prohibited. The system automatically quarantines expired batches from the active sales queue to ensure full DRAP compliance.

Q: How to recover from a computer crash?

Remedy: Copy your latest downloaded pharmadist-backup.db file into the prisma/ folder as wmdms.db. Restart the app to resume work.
