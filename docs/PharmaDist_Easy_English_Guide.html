PharmaDist ERP — Complete Abbreviations Glossary & LAN Network Setup Page 2

1 All Short Forms with Full Names & Plain-English Meaning

In pharmaceutical wholesale distribution, several abbreviations are used. Here is what every term means in plain, simple English:

Short Form Full Name What It Means in Plain English
TP Trade Price The manufacturer's wholesale price at which distributors purchase and bill to pharmacies.
MRP Maximum Retail Price The consumer price printed on the medicine box for retail patients.
GRN Goods Receipt Note The official warehouse document recorded when factory consignments arrive (Stock-In).
FEFO First-Expire, First-Out The system automatically picks the earliest-expiring batch first to eliminate expired stock losses.
FIFO First-In, First-Out When receiving customer payments, the oldest unpaid bill is cleared first.
AR Accounts Receivable Total credit money owed to your agency by pharmacies and medical stores.
AP Accounts Payable Total unpaid debt your agency owes to pharma manufacturers and vendors.
COGS Cost of Goods Sold The exact original purchase cost of the items sold, used to calculate true profit.
DRAP Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan The federal government regulatory body governing pharmaceutical licensing, sales, and labeling.
NTN / STRN National Tax No. / Sales Tax Reg. No. Official FBR tax registration numbers printed on legal wholesale invoices.
SKU Stock Keeping Unit The unique catalog code assigned to each medicine in the database.
P&L Profit and Loss The financial accounting statement showing business Gross and Net Profit.
RCT / INV Receipt / Invoice RCT = Payment collection voucher serial; INV = Wholesale sales invoice serial.

PharmaDist ERP — Initial Setup: Company Profile, Warehouses & Medicine Formulary Page 3

3 Initial Setup (One-Time Configuration)

When starting the system for the first time, complete these 4 initial configuration steps:

1
Company Profile & DRAP Wholesale License Setup
URL: /settings
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Administration → System Settings → Tab 1: General

Enter your registered wholesale firm details that will appear on all printed invoices and delivery challans:

Company Name: Registered agency name (e.g. Al-Noor Pharma Distributors)
DRAP License No: Wholesale license (e.g. DRAP/WDL/LHR-4921)
NTN / STRN: FBR tax registration numbers
Warehouse Address: Complete godown street address and phone number

Click the blue button at bottom-right: "Save Changes".

2
Warehouse (Godown) & Storage Racks Architecture
URL: /warehouses
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Inventory → Warehouses → Top-Right: + New Warehouse

Create your storage facility name (e.g. Central Main Warehouse). Inside, designate shelf/rack locations (e.g. Rack A-01 (Antibiotics), Cold Chain #1 (Insulin)) so warehouse staff can locate items in seconds.

3
Manufacturers (Pharma Companies) & Suppliers Registry
URL: /manufacturers and /suppliers
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Manufacturers / Suppliers → Top-Right: + Add Supplier

Register licensed pharmaceutical companies (GSK, Abbott Laboratories, Getz Pharma, Searle). Input supplier contact names, phone numbers, NTN, and credit terms (e.g. 30 Days Net).

4
Medicines (Master Drug Catalog) Registration
URL: /medicines
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Medicines → Top-Right: + Add Medicine

Clicking the button opens a modal window. Fill in the medicine specifications:

Medicine Name: Augmentin 625mg Tablets or Panadol 500mg
Generic Formula: Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid
Manufacturer: Select company from dropdown (e.g. GSK)
Dosage Form: Tablet, Syrup, Injection, or Capsule
Trade Price (TP): Wholesale cost rate (e.g. Rs. 240.00)
Retail Price (MRP): Price printed on the box (e.g. Rs. 280.00)
Wholesale Discount: Standard wholesale discount (e.g. 5%)
Reorder Alert Level: Minimum box quantity for alerts (e.g. 50 Packs)

Click "Save Medicine". The medicine is now active in your catalog!

5
Customer Pharmacies & Credit Limit Governance
URL: /customers
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Customer Pharmacies → Top-Right: + Add Customer

Pharmacy Name: Al-Shifa Medicos or City Pharmacy

6
Sales Representatives (Order Bookers / Field Staff) Setup
URL: /distributors
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Sales Representatives → Top-Right: + Add Rep

Register field order booking agents and delivery staff. Assign specific market routes and monthly sales targets to track their recovery and commission performance.

4 Daily Wholesale Operational Workflow

7
Receiving Factory Shipments (Purchase Intake GRN)
URL: /purchases/new
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Procurement → Purchase Intake (PackagePlus Icon)

When delivery trucks arrive with factory consignments, open this intake form:

1. Select Supplier: Choose manufacturer (e.g. Getz Pharma)
2. Delivery Challan No: Manufacturer invoice number (e.g. DC-7864)
3. Select Medicine: Medicine name (e.g. Augmentin 625mg)
4. Batch Number: Batch printed on carton (e.g. BT-9942)
5. Expiry Date: Expiry printed on carton (e.g. 2028-06-30)
6. Quantity Received: Number of boxes received (e.g. 500 Packs)
7. Unit Purchase Cost: Actual cost rate per box (e.g. Rs. 200.00)
8. Warehouse Rack: Physical rack coordinate (e.g. Rack B-04)

Click the blue button: "Submit Purchase Consignment".

✨ Immediate Updates: 500 boxes are added to your warehouse stock, Batch BT-9942 is registered, and the supplier's balance in Accounts Payable (AP) updates instantly.
PharmaDist ERP — Wholesale Sales, FEFO Engine, Invoicing & Debt Recovery Page 5
8
Wholesale Order Booking (Strict FEFO Batch Engine)
URL: /sales/new
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Wholesale Sales → New Sale Order (ShoppingCart Icon)

When a pharmacy places an order or an order booker returns from the market:

1. Customer: Al-Shifa Medicos (Live Credit Gauge displays current debt)
2. Sales Rep: Select booking agent
3. Add Medicine: Choose medicine (e.g. Augmentin 625mg)
4. Quantity: Number of boxes (e.g. 50 Packs)
⚡ FEFO Auto-Allocation Advantage: You do NOT have to search for batches manually! PharmaDist automatically selects the earliest expiring valid batch in your godown to ensure no expired stock ever gets stranded on your shelves.

Click "Confirm & Create Sale Order".

9
Printing DRAP Form 2-A Tax Invoices & Delivery Challans
URL: /invoices
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Wholesale Sales → Tax Invoices → Action Column: Print Icon (🖨️)

In the invoice table, click the Print icon next to any bill. A modal opens with two dedicated buttons:

📄 Print Tax Invoice (A4 Standard)

Includes Batch Number, Expiry Date, TP, MRP, wholesale discounts, tax, and authorized signature seals. Given to the pharmacy customer.

🚚 Print Delivery Challan

Omits commercial financial prices. Contains only quantities, item names, and batch numbers for delivery drivers and stock dispatchers.

10
Market Recovery Collections & Printable Money Receipts (RCT)
URL: /payments
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Finance & Accounts → Collections & Receipts → Top-Right: + Record Collection

When salesmen return with cash or cheques collected from pharmacies:

Select Customer: Al-Shifa Medicos (Displays total pending debt)
Amount Received: Amount collected (e.g. Rs. 50,000)
Payment Method: Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer, or Raast
Collected By: Sales representative who submitted funds

Click "Confirm Collection".

✨ FIFO Automated Debt Clearance: The system automatically clears the customer's oldest pending invoices first and opens a printable Money Receipt (RCT-2026-0001) for customer issuance.

Database Backup Download & Instant Disaster Recovery
URL: /settings (Tab 8)
📍 Screen Location: Left Sidebar Menu → Administration → System Settings → Tab 8: Backup & Maintenance

Click "Download Live Database (.db)". Save the file to an external USB flash drive or secure storage.

🛠️ Disaster Recovery Runbook (If Server Hardware Fails): On your replacement computer, simply copy your backup file into the prisma/ folder, rename it to wmdms.db, and launch the application. In less than 60 seconds, 100% of transactions, ledgers, and inventory history are fully restored!
