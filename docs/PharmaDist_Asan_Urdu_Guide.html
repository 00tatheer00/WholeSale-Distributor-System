PharmaDist ERP — Tamam Short Forms Ki Full Forms Aur LAN Setup Page 2

1 Tamam Short Forms Ki Full Forms Aur Asan Matlab

Pharma distribution mein jo mukhtasir alfaaz (abbreviations) istemal hotay hain, unka asan matlab yeh hai:

Short Form Full Form (Poora Naam) Asan Roman Urdu Mein Matlab
TP Trade Price Factory ki wholesale qeemat (jis rate par agency dawai khareedti ya pharmacy ko deti hai).
MRP Maximum Retail Price Dhabbe par chappi hui qeemat (jis rate par medical store mareez ko bechta hai).
GRN Goods Receipt Note Factory se consignments godown mein dakhil karne ki raseed (Stock-In).
FEFO First-Expire, First-Out Jo dawai pehle expire hogi, system pehle usi ko bechega taake godown mein expire na ho.
FIFO First-In, First-Out Recovery aane par customer ka sab se purana bill pehle clear karna.
AR Accounts Receivable Pharmacies / Customers par kul udhaar jo humne market se wasool karna hai.
AP Accounts Payable Factory ya medicine company ko jo baqaya paise humne ada karne hain.
COGS Cost of Goods Sold Bikay huay maal ki asal khareed qeemat (asal lagat).
DRAP Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan Pakistan mein dawaiyon aur pharmaceutical licensing ka sarkari idara.
NTN / STRN National Tax No. / Sales Tax Reg. No. FBR ka tax registration number jo legal wholesale invoice par chapta hai.
SKU Stock Keeping Unit Har dawai ka system ke andar unique pehchan code.
P&L Profit and Loss Karobar ka asal munafa aur nuqsan ka hisaab kitab.
RCT / INV Receipt / Invoice RCT = Payment wasooli raseed ka code; INV = Wholesale sale bill ka code.

PharmaDist ERP — Pehle Din Ka Setup: Company, Godown, Factories & Dawaiyan Page 3

3 Pehle Din Ka Setup (One-Time Configuration)

Software shuru karne ke baad sab se pehle yeh 4 cheezein set karni hain:

1
Company Profile Aur DRAP Wholesale License Setup
URL: /settings
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Administration → System Settings → Tab 1: General

Yahan apni wholesale agency ka official data enter karein jo print hone walay har bill par aayega:

Company Name: Apni wholesale agency ka naam (e.g. Al-Noor Pharma Distributors)
DRAP License No: Wholesale license (e.g. DRAP/WDL/LHR-4921)
NTN / STRN: FBR tax registration number
Warehouse Address: Godown ka mukammal pata aur phone number

Neeche right side par neelay button "Save Changes" par click karein.

2
Warehouse (Godown) Aur Racks Structure Banana
URL: /warehouses
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Inventory → Warehouses → Top-Right: + New Warehouse

Yahan apne godown ka naam banayein (maslan Central Main Warehouse). Iske andar shelves aur racks (maslan Rack A-01 (Antibiotics), Cold Chain #1 (Insulin)) banayein taake staff ko pata ho dawai kis rack mein padi hai.

3
Manufacturers (Pharma Companies) Aur Suppliers Register Karna
URL: /manufacturers aur /suppliers
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Manufacturers / Suppliers → Top-Right: + Add Supplier

Factory companies (GSK, Abbott Laboratories, Getz Pharma, Searle) ko register karein. Supplier ka naam, phone number, NTN, aur payment terms (jaise 30 din ka udhaar) likh kar save karein.

4
Medicines (Dawaiyon Ka Master Catalog) Add Karna
URL: /medicines
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Medicines → Top-Right: + Add Medicine

Button dabate hi pop-up khulay ga. Yahan dawai ka basic profile banayein:

Medicine Name: Augmentin 625mg Tablets ya Panadol 500mg
Generic Name (Formula): Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid
Manufacturer: Dropdown se company chunein (e.g. GSK)
Dosage Form: Tablet, Syrup, Injection, ya Capsule
Trade Price (TP): Factory wholesale rate (e.g. Rs. 240.00)
Retail Price (MRP): Dhabbe par likhi qeemat (e.g. Rs. 280.00)
Wholesale Discount: Aam discount percentage (e.g. 5%)
Reorder Alert Level: Dhabbon ki tadad jab alert aaye (e.g. 50 Packs)

Click karein "Save Medicine". Dawai master list mein register ho gayi!

PharmaDist ERP — Customers, Order Bookers Aur Factory Se Maal Lene Ka Tareeqa Page 4
5
Customer Pharmacies (Medical Stores) & Credit Limit Setup
URL: /customers
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Customer Pharmacies → Top-Right: + Add Customer

Medical stores aur clinics register karein jinko wholesale maal supply karna hai:

Pharmacy Name: Al-Shifa Medicos ya City Pharmacy
Drug License No: Store ka license (e.g. DL-2026-9812)
Owner Mobile / Phone: Raabta number (e.g. 0300-1234567)
Assigned Route: Area (e.g. Jail Road, Mall Road, Saddar)
Credit Limit (PKR): Udhaar ki hadh (e.g. Rs. 250,000)
Credit Max Days: Kitne din ka udhaar (e.g. 15 ya 30 Days)
⚠️ Credit Limit Engine: Agar Al-Shifa Medicos ka udhaar Rs. 250,000 se barh jaye, toh bill banate waqt system foran warning dega aur bill ko manager ke approval ke baghair aagay nahi jane dega.
6
Sales Representatives (Order Bookers / Field Staff) Setup
URL: /distributors
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Master Data → Sales Representatives → Top-Right: + Add Rep

Order bookers ke naam, mobile number, assigned market route, aur monthly sales recovery target enter karein taake har salesman ka commission aur performance check ho sakay.

4 Rozana Ka Karobari Chakar (Daily Operations)

7
Factory Se Maal Dakhil Karna (Purchase Intake GRN)
URL: /purchases/new
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Procurement → Purchase Intake (PackagePlus Icon)

Jab factory se gari aaye aur dhabbe godown mein utarein, toh yeh screen kholen:

1. Select Supplier: Dropdown se factory chunein (e.g. Getz Pharma)
2. Delivery Challan No: Factory invoice number (e.g. DC-7864)
3. Select Medicine: Dawai select karein (e.g. Augmentin 625mg)
4. Batch Number: Dhabbe par likha batch (e.g. BT-9942)
5. Expiry Date: Dhabbe ki expiry date (e.g. 2028-06-30)
6. Quantity Received: Kitne dhabbe aaye (e.g. 500 Packs)
7. Unit Purchase Cost: Factory se kitne ka mila (e.g. Rs. 200.00)
8. Warehouse Rack: Godown ki location (e.g. Rack B-04)

Neeche right par neelay button "Submit Purchase Consignment" par click karein.

✨ Kia Tabdeeli Hui? Godown stock mein 500 dhabbe foran shamil ho gaye, batch BT-9942 register ho gaya, aur Accounts Payable (AP) mein factory ke baqaya paise darj ho gaye.
PharmaDist ERP — Wholesale Sale, FEFO Engine, Bill Print Aur Cash Recovery Page 5
8
Wholesale Order Book Karna (Strict FEFO Engine)
URL: /sales/new
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Wholesale Sales → New Sale Order (ShoppingCart Icon)

Jab medical store se order aaye ya salesman booking laye:

1. Customer: Al-Shifa Medicos (Saamne Credit Limit Gauge aa jayega)
2. Sales Rep: Order laane wala salesman chunein
3. Add Medicine: Dawai chunein (e.g. Augmentin 625mg)
4. Quantity: Kitne dhabbe chahiye (e.g. 50 Packs)
⚡ FEFO Ka Jaadoo: Aapko batch dhoondne ki zaroorat nahi! System godown ke tamam batches mein se khud ba khud sab se qareeb expire hone wala batch pick karega taake purana maal pehle bikay aur expiry ka nuqsan na ho.

Click karein "Confirm & Create Sale Order".

9
DRAP Form 2-A Tax Invoice Aur Delivery Challan Print Karna
URL: /invoices
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Wholesale Sales → Tax Invoices → Action Column: Print Icon (🖨️)

Invoice list mein apne bill ke aagay Print icon dabayein. Pop-up khulay ga jisme do buttons hain:

📄 Print Tax Invoice (A4 Size)

Isme Batch No, Expiry Date, TP, MRP, Wholesale Discount, Tax, Total Dues aur legal signatures hotay hain. Yeh pharmacy ko diya jata hai.

🚚 Print Delivery Challan

Isme keemat NAHI hoti. Sirf dhabbon ki tadad, dawai ka naam aur batch number hota hai taake delivery boy maal theek deliver kar sakay.

10
Market Recovery Collections & Printable Money Receipt (RCT)
URL: /payments
📍 Screen Par Kahan Hai: Left Sidebar Menu → Finance & Accounts → Collections & Receipts → Top-Right: + Record Collection

Jab salesman market se cash ya cheque wasool karke aaye:

Select Customer: Al-Shifa Medicos (Unka kul udhaar nazar aayega)
Amount Received: Kitne paise wasool huay (e.g. Rs. 50,000)
Payment Method: Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer, ya Raast / JazzCash
Collected By: Salesman ka naam jisne paise jama karwaye

Click karein "Confirm Collection".

✨ FIFO Settlement: System khud ba khud customer ke sab se purane unpaid bills ko pehle clear karega aur foran Print Money Receipt (RCT-2026-0001) khol dega jo aap customer ko issue kar sakte hain.
