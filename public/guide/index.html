Select Supplier & Godown

Automated earliest-expiry batch selection and instant invoice printing

Jab kisi medical store, clinic ya hospital ko dawa bechni ho, toh Dashboard se sabz button + New Sale Bill dabayein. Yahan 3 asaan steps hain:

To invoice medicines to a pharmacy or clinic, click the green + New Sale Bill button on your Dashboard. Follow these 3 easy steps:

Create Sale Invoice & Bill (+ Naya Bill)

Step 1: Customer Pharmacy Select Karein

Medicine Stock & Batches Cockpit
Tasveer 5:Figure 5: Inventory Cockpit jahan neela button "+ Add New Stock" aur peela button "± Correct Stock / Damage" ooper hi diye gaye hain. Inventory Cockpit with "+ Add New Stock" and "± Correct Stock / Damage" buttons right at the top.
1

Agar Koi Shishi Toot Gayi Ya Kharab Ho Gayi:

Recording Breakage or Damaged Goods:

Ooper peela button ± Correct Stock / Damage dabayein. Dawa aur uska batch number choose karein. Waja likhein (maslan: "Peti me 2 bottle tooti hui nikleen"). Taadad me -2 likhein aur submit karein. System foran inventory ko durust kar dega.

Click the amber button ± Correct Stock / Damage at the top. Select the medicine and batch, type the reason (e.g., "Broken bottles found in box"), set the quantity to negative (e.g. -2), and submit. Inventory updates immediately.

2

Near Expiry Watchdog (Surkh Alerts):

Expiry Alerts (<90 Days):

Cockpit par Near Expiry (<90d) aur Expired Batches ke boxes diye gaye hain. Agar koi dawa 90 din me expire hone wali hogi toh system pehle hi alert de dega taake aap usay discount par foran nikaal sakein ya factory ko wapis kar sakein.

Batches nearing expiry within 90 days appear in the Near Expiry alert box, enabling you to sell them at promo rates or arrange return-to-vendor (RTV) before expiry.
