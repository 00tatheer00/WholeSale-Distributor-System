Khanay Ka Naam Field Name Matlab & Ahmiyat Purpose & DRAP Standard
Batch # Dawa ki peti par likha code (maslan B-101). DRAP qanoon ke mutabiq har dawa ka batch alag track hota hai. Manufacturer packaging lot code. Strictly tracked for recall and safety per DRAP wholesale standards.
Expiry Date Dawa ke khatam hone ki tareekh. FEFO algorithm is tareekh ko dekh kar pehle expire hone wali dawa pehle bechta hai. Shelf-life expiration date. Guides automated FEFO queuing to prevent stock obsolescence.
Bonus Qty Factory ki scheme ke teht milnay wale muft dabbay (e.g., 100 khareede toh 10 free mile). Free promotional bonus goods received under manufacturer trade schemes (e.g. 100 + 10 free).
Cost Price Distributor ko kitne rupay ki pari. Yeh raqam aap ke asal munafay (Gross Profit) ke hisab ke liye zaroori hai. Actual purchase acquisition cost. Preserved historically to calculate true gross profit margins.
Trade Price (TP) Wholesale market rate jis par customer pharmacy ko becha jata hai. Standard DRAP wholesale price invoiced to customer retail pharmacies.

Quantity & Waja (Reason) Likhein

Specify Quantity & Reason

Agar shishi toot gayi: Taadad me -2 likhein aur Reason me likhein "Peti me 2 bottle tooti hui nikleen".
