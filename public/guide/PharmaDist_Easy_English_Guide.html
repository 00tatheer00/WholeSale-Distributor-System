PharmaDist ERP — Easy English User Manual

A simple, step-by-step visual operating guide designed for pharmaceutical wholesale distributors, warehouse supervisors, and order clerks. Every routine operation is explained in 1-2-3 steps with real screen illustrations.

1

Dashboard Cockpit & 6 Quick Action Buttons

Everything is 1-click away — No complex menu hunting required

When you log into PharmaDist ERP, you arrive at the Wholesale Distribution Cockpit. At the very top, you have 6 vibrant Quick Action Cards that allow you to create or record any transaction with a single click:

Wholesale Distribution Cockpit (Dashboard)
Figure 1: Main Dashboard featuring the 6 Quick Action cards and Pakistani Rupee (Rs. / PKR) live figures.
🛒

+ New Sale Bill (Green Card)

Sell medicines to client pharmacies and print instant DRAP wholesale tax invoices.

📦

+ Add Factory Stock (Blue Card)

Enter new consignments received from pharmaceutical manufacturers with batch numbers and expiries.

🏪

+ Add Pharmacy (Purple Card)

Register new customer retail pharmacies, hospitals, or clinics and set their credit limit.

🏭

+ Add Supplier (Amber Card)

Register drug manufacturers, pharmaceutical suppliers, or authorized vendors.

🏢

+ Add Godown (Teal Card)

Configure storage godowns, cold storage rooms, or quarantine warehouses.

💸

+ Record Expense (Red Card)

Log warehouse rent, electricity bills, delivery vehicle fuel, tea, and staff salaries.

💡
Key Benefit: You don't need to hunt through sidebars. Over 95% of routine daily wholesale tasks are directly accessible from these 6 cards!
2

How to Change Your Admin Email & Password

Instant direct self-service in Settings — Zero technical assistance needed

If you want to update your system login email address or set a new password, open the Settings screen:

System Settings & Administration — Tab 1 (Business Profile)
Figure 2: Top card in Settings: "Admin Login Email & Password (Apna Login & Password Badlein)".
1

Open Settings

Click on System Settings at the bottom of the left sidebar.

2

Enter New Email or Password

Right at the top, you will see the Admin Login Email & Password card. Type your desired email address and enter your new password (minimum 6 characters) in both password fields.

3

Click Save

Click the blue Save My Email & Password button. A green confirmation badge will appear and your new login details are instantly active!

🔑
Tip: If you only want to change your email address and keep your current password, simply leave the two password fields blank and click Save!
3

Entering Factory Stock (Purchase Intake & Batches)

Receive shipments, record batch numbers, expiry dates, and assign to godowns

When pharmaceutical shipments arrive from manufacturers, follow this procedure to record them into inventory:

Direct Purchase Intake & Batch Creation
Figure 3: Purchase intake form with inline "+ New Supplier" and "+ New Godown" quick-creation buttons.
1

Open the Intake Form

Click the blue card + Add Factory Stock on your Dashboard (or navigate to Factory Purchase in sidebar).

2

Select Supplier & Godown

Select the drug manufacturer. If the supplier is not in the list, simply click the + New Supplier button right next to the dropdown to create it in 10 seconds! Select the destination warehouse (an inline + New Godown button is also available).

3

Enter Factory Invoice Number

Type the invoice or consignment delivery challan number provided by the factory.

4

Enter Medicine, Batch # and Expiry Date

Select the drug item. Enter the Batch Number printed on the packaging (e.g. BX-2026-01), pick the Expiry Date using the calendar, and specify the received quantity and bonus units.

5

Enter Cost Price & Trade Price (TP)

Enter your purchase acquisition cost (Cost Rs.) and the wholesale trade price (TP Rs.).

6

Commit Stock

Click Save & Receive Factory Stock at the bottom. The stock immediately updates your warehouse inventory and credits the supplier ledger.

Field Name Purpose & DRAP Standard
Batch # Manufacturer lot code. Strictly tracked for recall and safety per DRAP wholesale standards.
Expiry Date Shelf-life expiration date. Guides automated FEFO queuing to prevent stock obsolescence.
Bonus Qty Free promotional bonus goods received under manufacturer trade schemes (e.g. 100 + 10 free).
Cost Price Actual acquisition cost in PKR. Preserved historically to calculate true gross profit margins.
Trade Price (TP) Standard DRAP wholesale price invoiced to customer retail pharmacies.

Booking Wholesale Sales & Automatic FEFO Allocation

Automated earliest-expiry batch selection and instant invoice printing

To invoice medicines to a pharmacy or clinic, click the green + New Sale Bill button on your Dashboard. Follow these 3 easy steps:

Create Sale Invoice & Bill (+ Naya Bill)
Figure 4: 3-Step Sale Screen with inline "+ New Customer" button and green "FEFO (Earliest Expiry)" allocation badge.
1

Step 1: Choose Customer Pharmacy

Select the client pharmacy from the dropdown. If this is a new customer, click + New Customer right above the dropdown to register them instantly without leaving the form. Select the sales representative if applicable.

2

Step 2: Add Medicines & Quantity (FEFO Automatic)

Pick the required medicine. The system automatically pre-selects the batch that expires earliest (indicated by the prominent green FEFO (Earliest Expiry) badge). Enter order quantity and any trade discount percentage.

3

Step 3: Save & Print Invoice

Click the green button Create & Print Sale Invoice. A formatted DRAP wholesale tax bill and vehicle delivery challan will open ready for immediate printing!

🛡️
Credit Limit Protection: If a customer's unpaid balance exceeds their authorized credit limit, the system flags a warning to prevent unrecoverable bad debts.

7

Damaged Goods, Breakage & Stock Correction

How to reconcile broken bottles, expired losses, or physical audit variances

1

Recording Breakage or Damaged Goods:

Click the amber button ± Correct Stock / Damage at the top. Select the medicine and batch, type the reason (e.g., "Broken bottles found in box"), set the quantity to negative (e.g. -2), and submit. Inventory updates immediately.

2

Physical Recount Surpluses:

Click the same button, enter a positive quantity (e.g. +5), and record the physical audit reason.

8

Recording Operating Expenses (Overhead Costs)

Rent, electricity, fuel, delivery, salaries, and daily tea expenses

Daily Expenses & Bills (+ Record Kharcha)
Figure 7: Expenses screen tracking operational cost heads and net derivations.
1

Click Expense Button:

Click the red card + Record Expense directly on the Dashboard.

2

Choose Category:

Select the cost head: Warehouse Rent, Utilities & Electricity, Delivery & Fuel, or Staff Welfare / Tea.

3

Enter Amount & Save:

Enter the paid amount (e.g. Rs. 1,500) and save. This expense is deducted automatically from your gross profit to compute true Today's Net Profit.

Daily Distributor Routine (Standard Operating Procedure)

Morning opening, operational hours, and evening financial closing

🌅

Morning (08:00 - 09:00):

Open Dashboard. Check Low Stock and Near Expiry alerts. Print customer collection recovery sheets for your field salesmen.

☀️

Operational Hours:

Receive incoming factory consignments using + Add Factory Stock. Book pharmacy sales with + New Sale Bill and hand delivery challans to drivers.

🌙

Evening Closing (17:00 - 18:00):

Record money collected by field salesmen under Collections & Receipts. Log daily expenses with + Record Expense. Verify Today's Net Profit on the Dashboard before closing!
