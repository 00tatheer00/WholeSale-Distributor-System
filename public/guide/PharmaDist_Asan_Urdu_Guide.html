PharmaDist ERP — Asan Roman Urdu Guide

Yeh guide khaas tor par medicine wholesale distributors, godown in-charge aur munshi hazraat ke liye likhi gayi hai. Har step aasan zaban me tafseel aur asli screenshots ke saath samjhaya gaya hai taake koi bhi shakhs bina kisi mushkil ke system chala sake.

1

Dashboard Aur 6 Jadoo-i Quick Action Buttons

Her cheez 1-Click ke faslay par — Koi bhi cheez dhoondne ki zaroorat nahi

Jab aap PharmaDist ERP login karte hain toh sab se pehle Dashboard (Cockpit) samnay aata hai. Dashboard ke bilkul ooper 6 chamakdar rangon ke Quick Action Cards diye gaye hain jin par click karke aap foran koi bhi naya record dakhil kar sakte hain:

Wholesale Distribution Cockpit (Dashboard)
Tasveer 1: Dashboard par 6 barray buttons aur Pakistan Currency (Rs. / PKR) live figures.
🛒

+ New Sale Bill (Sabz Card)

Customer medical store ko dawa bechne aur foran invoice print karne ke liye yahan click karein.

📦

+ Add Factory Stock (Neela Card)

Dawa banane wali factory se jo naya stock aur batch godown me aye usay dakhil karein.

🏪

+ Add Pharmacy (Jamni Card)

Nayi customer pharmacy, medical store ya clinic ko register karein aur credit limit set karein.

🏭

+ Add Supplier (Pila Card)

Nayi pharmaceutical company ya vendor ko system me dakhil karein.

🏢

+ Add Godown (Ferozi Card)

Naya godown, warehouse, cold-storage ya kamra add karein.

💸

+ Record Expense (Surkh Card)

Dukan ka kiraya, bijli ka bill, petrol, chai-pani aur tankhwah ka kharcha dakhil karein.

💡
Faidah: Aap ko left side ke lambay menu me jane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Dashboard ke in 6 buttons se 95% rozana ka kaam foran ho jata hai!
2

Apna Login Email Aur Password Kaise Badlein?

Settings me Direct Access — Kisi software engineer ki zaroorat nahi

Agar aap apna login email ya password tabdeel karna chahte hain toh Settings ke pehle hi page par sab se ooper option majood hai:

System Settings & Administration — Tab 1 (Business Profile)
Tasveer 2: Settings kholte hi sab se ooper neela card "Admin Login Email & Password" nazar aata hai.
1

Settings Page Kholein

Left side ke menu me sab se neechay System Settings par click karein.

2

Naya Email Ya Password Type Karein

Sab se pehla card Admin Login Email & Password hai. Wahan apna naya email likhein aur New Password ke khanay me naya password type karein (kam az kam 6 characters).

3

Save Button Dabayein

Neela button Save My Email & Password dabayein. Foran green message aayega aur aap ka naya password active ho jayega!

🔑
Important Note: Agar aap sirf email badalna chahte hain aur password wahi purana rakhna chahte hain, toh password ke dono khanay khali chorh dein aur Save dabayein!
3

Factory Se Stock Dakhil Karna (Purchase Intake & Batches)

Nayi peti receive karein, Batch aur Expiry likhein aur godown me stock add karein

Jab dawa banane wali factory ya supplier se gari aye aur peti godown me dakhil ho, toh stock dakhil karne ka aasan tareeqa yeh hai:

Direct Purchase Intake & Batch Creation
Tasveer 3: Purchase screen me Supplier aur Godown ke saath inline "+ New" buttons diye gaye hain.
1

Form Kholein

Dashboard par neelay card + Add Factory Stock par click karein (ya menu se Factory Purchase par jayein).

2

Supplier & Godown Select Karein

Dawa banane wali company select karein. Agar company list me nahi hai, toh dropdown ke saath hi + New Supplier ka button dabayein aur 10 second me company add karein! Godown select karein (saath hi + New Godown button bhi majood hai).

3

Supplier Invoice # Likhein

Factory ki taraf se aane wale invoice ya delivery chalan ka number likhein.

4

Dawa, Batch Number Aur Expiry Likhein

Dawa select karein. Peti par likha Batch Number (maslan BX-2026-01) likhein. Expiry Date calendar se select karein. Kitni taadad (Qty) aayi aur kitna Bonus mila likhein.

5

Cost Price Aur Trade Price (TP) Likhein

Aap ko dawa kitne ki mili (Cost Rs.) aur market me kitne ki bechni hai (TP Rs.) likhein.

6

Stock Save Karein

Form ke aakhir me Save & Receive Factory Stock dabayein. Stock foran aap ke inventory me dakhil ho jayega aur company ka khata update ho jayega!

Khanay Ka Naam Matlab & Kaam
Batch # Dawa ki peti par likha code. DRAP qanoon ke mutabiq har batch alag track hota hai.
Expiry Date Dawa ki khatam hone ki tareekh. System is ko dekh kar FEFO queue chalata hai.
Bonus Qty Factory ki scheme ke muft dabbay (e.g. 100 + 10 free).
Cost Price Aap ko dawa kitne ki pari. Asal gross profit ke liye zaroori hai.
Trade Price (TP) Wholesale rate jis par medical store ko becha jata hai.
4

Customer Ko Dawa Bechna Aur Naya Bill Banana (FEFO Sale)

Automatic FEFO (Pehle Expire Hone Wala Pehle Bechein) aur instant bill print

Jab kisi medical store, clinic ya hospital ko dawa bechni ho, toh Dashboard se sabz button + New Sale Bill dabayein. Yahan 3 asaan steps hain:

Create Sale Invoice & Bill (+ Naya Bill)
Tasveer 4: 3-Step Sale Screen jahan Customer dropdown ke saath "+ New Customer" button aur FEFO Earliest Expiry badge nazar aata hai.
1

Step 1: Customer Pharmacy Select Karein

Medical Store ka naam select karein. Agar medical store pehli dafa aayi hai toh dropdown ke upar + New Customer button dabayein aur foran register karein (page se bahar jane ki zaroorat nahi!). Salesman / Order Booker bhi select karein.

2

Step 2: Dawa Aur Quantity Select Karein (FEFO Magic!)

Dawa select karein. System khud-b-khud woh batch samnay layega jis ki expiry sab se qareeb hai (saath sabz rang ka FEFO (Earliest Expiry) badge laga hoga!). Taadad (Qty) likhein aur agar discount dena ho toh Disc% likhein.

3

Step 3: Save Karein & Invoice Print Karein

Sabz button Create & Print Sale Invoice dabayein. Foran computer se DRAP standard ka Wholesale Tax Bill aur Driver Delivery Chalan print nikal aayega!

🛡️
Khatay Ka Tahaffuz (Credit Limit Hold): Agar kisi medical store ka udhar pehle se bohot zyada ho chuka hai aur uski limit paar ho gayi hai, toh system foran red warning dega taake aap ka paisa na doobay!
5

Naya Customer / Medical Store Register Karna

Credit Limit, Drug License, Territory Route aur Contact details

Add New Customer Pharmacy
Tasveer 5: Customer onboarding screen jahan pharmacy name, phone, aur credit limit darj ki jati hai.
1

Pharmacy Ka Naam & Malik Ka Naam:

Dukan ka naam (maslan Al-Shifa Medical Store) aur malik ka naam darj karein.

2

Credit Limit (Rs.) & Credit Days:

Aap is customer ko kitna udhar dena chahte hain (maslan Rs. 100,000) aur kitne din ke liye (maslan 30 din). Agar udhar is hadd se barhega toh system khud order rokega.

6

Stock & Batches Control (Inventory Cockpit)

Live stock valuation, batch-by-batch expiry dates aur reorder alerts

Godown me kitni dawa bachi hai aur unki expiry kab hai, yeh dekhne ke liye left menu se Stock & Batches (Inventory) par jayein:

Medicine Stock & Batches Cockpit
Tasveer 6: Inventory Cockpit jahan neela button "+ Add New Stock" aur peela button "± Correct Stock / Damage" ooper hi diye gaye hain.
🔍

Live Search & Filter:

Search box me dawa ka naam ya batch number type karein. Foran live record samnay aa jayega.

⚠️

Near Expiry Watchdog (<90 Days):

Jo dawa 90 din me expire hone wali hogi, system usay pehle hi alert de dega taake aap usay discount par nikaal sakein ya factory wapis bhej sakein.

7

Tooti / Kharab Dawa Durust Karna (Damage & Correction)

Agar peti me shishi toot jaye ya ginti me farq aye toh stock kaise theek karein

1

Agar Koi Shishi Toot Gayi Ya Kharab Ho Gayi:

Inventory me ooper peela button ± Correct Stock / Damage dabayein. Dawa aur uska batch number choose karein. Waja likhein (maslan: "Peti me 2 bottle tooti hui nikleen"). Taadad me -2 likhein aur submit karein. System foran inventory ko durust kar dega.

2

Agar Ginti Me Ziyada Nikla:

Wahi button dabayein, taadad me +5 likhein aur reason "Ginti me baramad hua" likhein.

8

Rozana Ka Kharcha Dakhil Karna (Business Expenses)

Kiraya, Bijli, Petrol, Chai-Pani aur Staff Salaries

Daily Expenses & Bills (+ Record Kharcha)
Tasveer 7: Expenses screen jahan rozana ka kharcha categories me taqseem kiya jata hai.
1

Dashboard Se Button Dabayein

Dashboard par surkh rang ka card + Record Expense dabayein.

2

Category Choose Karein

Kharchay ki qisam select karein: Warehouse Rent (Kiraya), Utilities & Electricity (Bijli/Gas), Delivery & Fuel (Gari ka petrol), ya Staff Welfare (Chai-Pani).

3

Raqam Likhein & Save Karein

Kitne rupay kharch huye (maslan Rs. 1500) likhein aur Save daba dein. Yeh raqam foran aap ke munafay me se minus ho kar sahi Net Profit calculate karegi.

9

Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer ya Raast ke zariye recovery dakhil karna

1

Collections Menu Kholein

Sidebar se Collections & Receipts kholen aur + Record Payment dabayein.

2

Medical Store & Raqam Likhein

Customer pharmacy select karein (unka purana balance nazar aayega). Kitna paisa wasool hua likhein.

3

Money Receipt Print Karein

Save karne ke baad foran dastakhati Money Receipt print karein jo customer ko pohanch ke tor par di ja sake.

10

Supplier Ko Payment Dena (Factory Settlement)

Dawa banane wali company ka hisab kitab aur balance clearance

1

Suppliers Directory Kholein

Sidebar se Suppliers & Accounts par jayein. Har company ka baqaya wahan nazar aayega.

2

Payment Voucher Record Karein

Company ke naam par click karein aur + Make Payment daba kar jitna paisa diya likh kar save karein.

11

10 Centralized Reports & 1-Click Excel Export

Sales, Stock, Expiry, Low Stock aur Customer Udhar ke mukammal Excel reports

Sidebar se Reports & Analytics kholen. Yahan 10 ahem reports dastyab hain:

📊

Dastyab Reports:

1. Sales Report (Rozana aur mahana sales)
2. Inventory Valuation Report (Godown ke mal ki total qeemat)

1-Click Excel / CSV Export:

Har report ke ooper Export Excel (.xls) ka button diya gaya hai. Click karte hi poori sheet Excel me download ho jati hai!

12

Rozana Ka Kaam Band Karne Ka Schedule (Munshi SOP)

Subah, Dopahar aur Shaam ka aasan time table

🌅

Subah 8:00 se 9:00 baje:

Dashboard khol kar Low Stock aur Near Expiry alerts check karein. Salesmen ko unke ilaqa ke customers ki recovery list print karke dein.

☀️

Din ke doraan:

Factory se jo gari aye uska mal + Add Factory Stock se dakhil karein. Customer pharmacies ke orders + New Sale Bill se banayein aur driver ko delivery challan dein.

🌙

Shaam 5:00 se 6:00 baje (Hisab Kitab):

Salesmen se aane wala cash Collections & Receipts me dakhil karein. Din bhar ka kharcha + Record Expense me dalein. Dashboard par Today's Net Profit check karein aur system close karein!
