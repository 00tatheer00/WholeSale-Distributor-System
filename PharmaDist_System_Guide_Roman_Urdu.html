1 Retail POS Aur Wholesale ERP Mein Bunyadi Farq

PharmaDist aam retail medical store ka software nahi hai, balkay yeh Wholesale Pharmaceutical Distributors ke liye banaya gaya hai jahan poore dhabbe aur cartons supply hotay hain:

Pehloo Aam Retail POS (Medical Store) PharmaDist Wholesale ERP (Aapka System)

PharmaDist ERP — Master Setup Aur Inventory Intake Page 3

3 Step-by-Step Karobari Sequence (Steps 1 se 5)

1
Company Profile Aur DRAP Wholesale License Setup Settings → General

/settings par jayein. Apni agency ka registered naam, DRAP Wholesale Drug License No. (jaise DRAP/WDL/LHR-4921), NTN, STRN, godown ka mukammal pata aur bank account details enter karein. Yeh saari details automatically print hone walay Tax Invoices aur Delivery Challans par aayengi.

2
Warehouse (Godown) Aur Racks Structure Banana Inventory → Warehouses

Apne physical godowns configure karein. Central Godown banayein aur uske andar racks aur shelves define karein (maslan Rack A-01 (Antibiotics), Cold Chain Unit #2 (Insulins)). Is se order pack karte waqt staff ko foran pata chal jata hai ke dawai kahan padi hai.

3
Manufacturers Aur Factory Suppliers Add Karna Purchases → Suppliers

Licensed pharmaceutical multinational aur national companies (GSK, Abbott Laboratories, Getz Pharma, Searle, Highnoon) ko register karein. Unka NTN, payment credit terms (jaise 30 din ka udhaar), aur contact numbers darj karein.

4
Medicines (Dawaiyon Ka Master Catalog) Register Karna Medicines → Catalog

Dawaiyon ke master profiles banayein: Generic salt name, dosage form (Tablet, Syrup, Injection), Trade Price (TP), Maximum Retail Price (MRP), wholesale discount rate, aur minimum reorder alert limit set karein taake stock khatam hone se pehle alert milay.

5
Purchase Intake (Factory Se Maal Dakhil Karna - GRN) Purchases → New Consignment

Jab factory se shipment aaye, Supplier chunein, Delivery Challan number likhein, aur har dawai ka Manufacturer Batch Number, Manufacturing Date, Expiry Date, khareed qeemat, aur godown rack location select karein. Submit karte hi godown ka stock aur factory liability (AP) update ho jati hai.

⚠️ FEFO Expiry Guardrail: Purchase dakhil karte waqt hamesha dhabbe par likhi hui theek expiry date daalein. System ka automated FEFO algorithm isi date ki bunyad par sab se pehle expire hone wala dhabba sale ke liye pick karta hai.
PharmaDist ERP — Sales Execution & Financial Recovery Page 4
6
Customer Pharmacies & Credit Limit (Udhaar Ki Hadh) Customers → Directory

Medical stores, retail pharmacies, aur hospital procurement accounts add karein. Unka Pharmacy Drug License number, maalik ka mobile no, route, aur Credit Limit (jaise Rs. 300,000 aur 15 din ka udhaar) set karein.

7
Sales Representatives (Order Bookers / Field Staff) Sales Reps → Teams

Field order bookers aur delivery boys create karein. Har salesman ko unka market area/route aur mahana sales target assign karein taake unki recovery aur commission ka hisaab lagaya ja sake.

8
Wholesale Order Booking (Strict FEFO Batch Engine) Sales → New Wholesale Order

Pharmacy select karein. Screen par foran customer ka credit gauge nazar aayega ke kitna udhaar baqi hai. Jaise hi dawai chunein ge, system khud ba khud First-Expire First-Out (FEFO) ke mutabiq sab se pehle expire hone wala batch utha lega. Agar udhaar limit cross ho toh manager approval chahiye hota hai.

9
DRAP A4 Tax Invoice Aur Delivery Challan Print Karna Invoices → Print Engine

Print Invoice par click karein aur single-page A4 size ka mukammal Tax Invoice print ho jayega jisme Batch No, Expiry, TP, MRP, discount aur customer seal line hoti hai. Print Challan par click karne se delivery driver ke liye baghair keemat wala challan print hota hai.

10
Market Recovery Collections & Printable Money Receipt Payments → New Collection

Jab salesman market se paise laye, toh Cash, Cheque, ya Bank Transfer select karke amount dakhil karein. FIFO rule ke tehat purane pending bills pehle clear ho jayenge aur customer ke liye official printable Money Receipt (RCT) generate ho jayegi.

✨ Historical COGS Integrity: Har invoice sale ke waqt ki asal khareed qeemat (COGS) ko hamesha ke liye lock kar leti hai. Chahe 2 mahine baad factory rate barh bhi jaye, purana munafa 100% accurate aur verifiable rehta hai.

13
Reports Suite Aur Excel / CSV Data Export Reports → Reporting Center

9 specialized reports mojood hain: Customer Balances (AR), Supplier Liabilities (AP), Inventory Valuation, Batch Expiry Watchdog (30/60/90 din), Low Stock Reorder, aur Sales Rep Targets. Har report ko ek click par Excel (.xls) ya CSV mein download karein.

14
Database Snapshot & Disaster Recovery (Data Mehfooz Karna) Settings → Backup & Maintenance

Ek click par live SQLite database backup file (.db) apne computer ya USB drive par download karein. Agar computer kharab ho jaye, toh naye computer mein software daal kar backup file ko prisma/wmdms.db bana dein; 100% data foran wapas aa jayega!

15
Staff Team Governance Aur Tamper-Proof Audit Logs Audit Logs → Forensic Logs

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ke tehat har staff ka daera-e-kar alag hota hai. Tamam ahem actions (rate badalna, bill cancel karna, credit override) tamper-proof audit trail mein hamesha ke liye user ID aur time ke sath record hotay hain.

5 Khas Sawalaat Aur Asaan Jawabaat (FAQ)

Q: Order banate waqt "Stock Error" aye toh?

Hal: Is batch mein stock khatam hai. Purchases → New mein naya consignment dakhil karein ya doosra batch select karein.

Q: "Credit Limit Exceeded" alert ka matlab?

Hal: Us pharmacy ka udhaar limit se barh gaya hai. Pehle purana udhaar wasool karein ya Sales Manager se override approve karwayein.

Q: Kia expired dawai ka bill ban sakta hai?

Hal: Hargiz nahi! System expired batch ko sales queue se khud ba khud block kar deta hai taake koi ghalti na ho.

Q: Computer kharab ho jaye toh data kaise bachega?

Hal: Apni backup file ko naye computer ke prisma/ folder mein copy karein aur uska naam wmdms.db rakh kar app chala dein.
